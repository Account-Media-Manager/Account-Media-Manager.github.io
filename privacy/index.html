Privacy Policy

Account Media Manager · Last updated: 12 October 2026

1. Who we are

Account Media Manager ("the App") is a private tool operated by its owner to view analytics for, and manage content on, the owner's own social media accounts. The App is not offered to the public. Contact: your-email@example.com

2. What data the App accesses

When the owner connects a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the App can access only the data allowed by the permissions (scopes) the owner approves:

  • Profile information: display name, username, avatar, bio and verification status.
  • Account statistics: follower, following, likes and video counts.
  • Post information: a list of the account's videos with title, description, creation time, link, and view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Posting: where enabled, the App can send videos or photos to the connected account as drafts or posts.
